Welcome

This tool helps you move your book library out of Fable and into Goodreads. The whole process takes about five minutes.

What happens next

  1. We'll open a fresh browser window so you can log in to Fable.
  2. Once you're logged in, we'll read your library and bring it back here.
  3. You can review and tidy up the list, then download a file to upload to Goodreads.
