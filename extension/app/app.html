Welcome

ShelfBridge gets your reading library out of Fable, so you can take it wherever you like. The whole thing takes about five minutes.

What happens next

  1. We'll open a Fable tab so you can log in (or use one you already have open).
  2. We'll read your library and bring it back here for review.
  3. You'll pick where it's going, download a file, and upload it there.

You can send it to Goodreads, StoryGraph or Pagebound. Pick at the end, and you can come back and download for another one whenever you want.

      How to use ShelfBridge

      A complete walkthrough of getting your library out of Fable. Each section below corresponds to one of the three steps in the wizard.

      Step 1 — Connect to Fable

      Click "Connect to Fable" on the welcome screen. If you have a Fable tab open already, we'll use that one — otherwise we'll open one for you. If you're not signed in to Fable yet, sign in there, then come back to this tab and click Connect again.

      Privacy: ShelfBridge never sees your Fable password. You sign in on Fable's own page, in your normal browser session.

      Step 2 — Review your library

      Anything you click in the table becomes editable. Changes save automatically — there's no "save" button. You can:

      • Edit any cell — title, author, shelf, dates, rating, or ISBN.
      • Star ratings, including half stars: click the left half of a star for the half, the right half for the whole. Click the same spot again to clear it.
      • Filter by shelf — pill buttons above the table.
      • Delete a row — small × at the end of each row.

      What the "month only" badge means

      Fable only knew the month or year for some dates, not the exact day. We've defaulted the missing parts to "01" — you may want to correct the date manually. The badge disappears once you do.

      Step 3 — Pick where it's going

      Click Continue to export and choose a destination. Each one tells you what comes across and what doesn't, and gives you the steps for importing into that particular app.

      • Goodreads. The original route, and the one most people have used.
      • StoryGraph. Upload the file using StoryGraph's Goodreads import, because StoryGraph doesn't import its own files.
      • Pagebound. Upload the file using Pagebound's StoryGraph option. This is the only one that keeps half stars.

      Why the file has the wrong name: files are named after the format, not the app. So choosing StoryGraph gets you a file with "goodreads" in the name, and choosing Pagebound gets you one with "storygraph" in it. That's correct, and the export page explains it as you go. Upload them exactly as they are.

      Things to know about dates and ratings

      • Finish dates come across everywhere.
      • Start dates don't come across at all. None of these apps can import one, because none of the file formats they accept has a column for it. You'd have to add them by hand afterwards.
      • Half stars only survive on Pagebound. Goodreads can't store them, and StoryGraph rounds them on the way in even though it can.
      • Reviews don't transfer anywhere.

      Can I send my library to more than one place?

      Yes. Your library stays here after you download, so you can go back to the export page, pick a different destination and download again. Nothing is used up.

      Privacy

      Everything runs in your own browser. Your book data is stored locally via Chrome's extension storage — never sent to any server we run. The only external service is Fable's own API (api.fable.co), to read your shelves.
