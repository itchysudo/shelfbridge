How to import into Goodreads

  1. Open goodreads.com/review/import in a new tab.
  2. Click Choose File and pick the file you just downloaded.
  3. Click Import books and wait a minute or two.

A couple of things to know about the dates:

  • Date Read in Goodreads = your Fable finish date.
  • Date Added in Goodreads is mapped from your Fable start date — Goodreads has no "date started" column of its own. Books on your To-Read shelf will have a blank Date Added.
  • Reviews are not accepted by Goodreads' CSV importer — only ratings, shelves, and dates come across.

How to use ShelfBridge

A complete walkthrough of moving your library from Fable to Goodreads. Each section below corresponds to one of the three steps in the wizard.

Step 1 — Connect to Fable

Click "Connect to Fable" on the welcome screen. If you have a Fable tab open already, we'll use that one — otherwise we'll open one for you. If you're not signed in to Fable yet, sign in there, then come back to this tab and click Connect again.

Privacy: ShelfBridge never sees your Fable password. You sign in on Fable's own page, in your normal browser session.

Step 2 — Review your library

Anything you click in the table becomes editable. Changes save automatically — there's no "save" button. You can:

  • Edit any cell — title, author, shelf, dates, rating, or ISBN.
  • Star ratings — click a star to set, click the same star again to clear.
  • Filter by shelf — pill buttons above the table.
  • Delete a row — small × at the end of each row.

What the "month only" badge means

Fable only knew the month or year for some dates, not the exact day. We've defaulted the missing parts to "01" — you may want to correct the date manually. The badge disappears once you do.

Step 3 — Export & import to Goodreads

  1. Click Continue to export →, then Download Goodreads file.
  2. Open goodreads.com/review/import.
  3. Upload the downloaded file. Wait a minute or two while Goodreads processes it.

Things to know about dates and ratings

  • Date Read in Goodreads = your Fable finish date.
  • Date Added in Goodreads is mapped from your Fable start date — Goodreads' CSV format has no "date started reading" column.
  • To-Read books have a blank Date Added; Goodreads fills in today's date on import.
  • Reviews don't transfer. Ratings, shelves, and dates all come across.

Privacy

Everything runs in your own browser. Your book data is stored locally via Chrome's extension storage — never sent to any server we run. The only external service is Fable's own API (api.fable.co), to read your shelves.
